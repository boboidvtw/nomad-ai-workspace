> _Nomad AI Workspace — 1.3.0 Nomad AI Studio Desktop Workstation & 4-AI Concurrent Sync_

### Highlights

- 🖥️ **Nomad AI Studio Standalone Desktop Workstation**: Introducing the all-new Electron 40 desktop application (`packages/desktop`) integrating Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, and Grok into a unified, high-performance workstation.
- ⚡ **1-Click 4-AI Concurrent Prompt Sync**: Dispatch prompts to all selected AI engines simultaneously with intelligent auto-submit adapters tailored for each platform.
- 📐 **Dynamic Multi-View Grid**: Seamlessly toggle between Quad (4-split grid), Dual (2-column side-by-side), and Focus (single-column) layouts.
- 🛡️ **Zero-Trust Session Persistence**: Independent local storage session (`~/Library/Application Support/nomad-desktop`) ensures persistent logins without any proxy or intermediary cloud server.
- 🍎 **Native macOS Retina App**: Native `.app` bundles installed in `/Applications` and Desktop with modern geometric Retina squircle icon.

> _Nomad AI Workspace — 1.3.0 Nomad AI Studio 独立桌面工作站与四大 AI 同步提问_

### 亮点

- 🖥️ **Nomad AI Studio 独立桌面超级工作站**：全新推出基于 Electron 40 的独立桌面端（`packages/desktop`），同屏聚合 Claude、ChatGPT、Gemini 与 Grok 四大 AI。
- ⚡ **跨 AI 一键并发同步提问**：底部全局统一输入框，一键将同一 Prompt 同步填入选中的所有 AI 并自动提交执行。
- 📐 **动态多视窗网格布局**：支持 Quad（四分割九宫格）、Dual（双栏并排对比）与 Focus（单栏专注）三大布局秒级切换。
- 🛡️ **零信任会话持久化**：采用独立的本地持久化 Session（`~/Library/Application Support/nomad-desktop`），登录状态永久保留，无任何第三方中继代理。
- 🍎 **macOS 原生应用安装**：提供已编译的 `/Applications/Nomad AI Studio.app` 与桌面快捷启动，附带定制 Retina 高清圆角图标。
