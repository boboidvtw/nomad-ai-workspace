> _Nomad AI Workspace — 1.0.0 Official Release_

### Highlights

- 🧭 **Nomad AI Workspace Initial Launch**: Unified local-first workspace for Google Gemini, Anthropic Claude, OpenAI ChatGPT, and xAI Grok.
- 🗂️ **Hierarchical Multi-AI Sidebar**: Organize conversations across platforms in a clean nested tree view with official color badges and instant cross-platform jumping.
- ☁️ **Scheme A Google Drive Segregation**: Safe, multi-platform backup into dedicated subfolders (`Gemini/`, `Claude/`, `ChatGPT/`, `Shared/`) directly on your personal Drive with zero middleman.
- ⚡ **Universal Prompt Manager & Super Orb**: Slash commands (`/`), template variable fill dialogs (`{{var}}`), prompt pinning, and floating Nomad Super Orb.
- ⌨️ **Timeline Navigation & Shortcuts**: Vim-style `j`/`k`, `gg`/`GG` fast message jump, and `Cmd/Ctrl+Enter` send safeguards.

### Features

- **Full Multi-Platform Support**: Official support for Gemini & Claude with ready adapters for ChatGPT & Grok.
- **Advanced Scientific Rendering**: KaTeX math, Mermaid diagrams, WaveDrom timing charts, and ECharts visualization.
- **Clean Document Export**: Instant export to pure Markdown (.md), high-res PNG, or formatted PDF.
